Data deletion

Dragon Melt keeps no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. All game data is on your device.

To delete it, delete the game: touch and hold its icon on the Home Screen, choose Remove App, then Delete App. This removes your progress, your settings and any unused items for good. They cannot be brought back, by us or by Apple.

If the game is part of a device backup (iCloud or computer), that copy disappears when the backup is deleted or replaced by a newer one.

Records of your purchases are kept by Apple under your Apple Account. We have no access to them.

If you have written to us and want your emails deleted, ask at bayrambas255@icloud.com. We delete them and confirm.
