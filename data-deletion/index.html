Data deletion

Dragon Melt keeps no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. All game data is on your device.

To delete it, delete the game: touch and hold its icon, choose Remove App, then Delete App. This removes your progress and any unused items for good.

If the game is part of a device backup, that copy disappears when the backup is deleted or replaced by a newer one.
