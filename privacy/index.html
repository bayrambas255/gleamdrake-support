Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short

Dragon Melt does not collect personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who we are

Dragon Melt is published by Bayram Baş, an individual developer in Türkiye. Contact: bayrambas255@icloud.com.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, gold, gems, items and settings) is saved in a file on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. It can be part of your own device backup, which is covered by your agreement with Apple.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation that a purchase was completed and keeps it on your device so the items can be delivered.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device, for example when a chest is ready. They are created on the device itself. No push service is used and nothing is sent to a server.

Other parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or social software. Apple may process data about your device and your use of the App Store under its own policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

The game does not collect data from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Deleting the game removes all of its data from your device. We hold nothing about you, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. If the game ever starts to collect data, this page and the App Store privacy details are updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: bayrambas255@icloud.com.
