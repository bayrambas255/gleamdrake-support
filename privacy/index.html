1. In short

Dragon Melt does not collect personal data. It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Everything the game saves stays on your device. We learn something about you only if you choose to write to us.

2. Who we are

Dragon Melt is published by Bayram Baş, an individual developer in Türkiye, who is the data controller for the little that this policy describes. Contact: bayrambas255@icloud.com.

3. What the game saves on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, gold, gems, items, the dragon's lair and your settings) is saved in a file on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. It can be part of your own device backup (iCloud or computer), which is covered by your agreement with Apple.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple and charged to your Apple Account. We never see your name, your Apple Account, your card or any other payment detail.

The game receives a confirmation from Apple that a purchase was completed. It keeps the identifier of that purchase in the save file on your device, only so that the items are delivered exactly once. Apple sends us sales reports with totals (what was bought, and in which country or region), not who bought it.

5. Reminders

If you ask the game to notify you and allow notifications, it schedules reminders on your device: your lives are full, a chest is ready, the Treasury is full, the daily reward is waiting. They are created on the device itself. No push service is used and nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off at any time in the Settings app, under Notifications.

6. What the game does not do

  • No account, sign-in or profile.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifier.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting software, ours or anyone else's.
  • No tracking across apps and websites.
  • No use of your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • No connection to servers of ours: we do not run any.

7. Apple

The game is distributed through the App Store and runs on iOS, so Apple processes data about your device, your Apple Account and your purchases under its own privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy.

If you have chosen on your device to share analytics with app developers, Apple gives us summary statistics and crash reports that do not identify you. Ratings and reviews that you leave on the App Store are published by Apple.

8. When you write to us

If you send us an email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to answer you and to fix the problem you report. We do not add you to any list and we do not pass it on to anyone.

Our mailbox is provided by Apple (iCloud Mail), so the messages are stored on Apple's servers, which may be outside your country. We keep a message only as long as it is needed to help you, and we delete it sooner if you ask. Where the law asks for a legal ground, it is our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us.

9. This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records technical data such as your IP address when you open a page, under its own privacy statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement. The site itself sets no cookies, runs no scripts or analytics, and loads nothing from other companies.

10. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, children included. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If a child has written to us, a parent or guardian can ask us to delete the message. Parents can turn in-app purchases off, or approve each one with Ask to Buy, in the Screen Time and Family Sharing settings of the device.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, the law gives you rights over your personal data: for example the KVKK in Türkiye, the UK GDPR, the LGPD in Brazil, the CCPA in California, PIPA in South Korea and the APPI in Japan. They include the right to ask what we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to its use and to complain to the data protection authority of your country.

Since the game collects nothing, the only personal data we can hold is an email that you sent us. Write to bayrambas255@icloud.com and we will answer within 30 days. We do not sell or share personal information, and we make no automated decisions about anyone.

12. Deleting your data

Deleting the game removes all of its data from your device. The steps, and what happens to device backups, are on the Data deletion page.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. If the game ever starts to collect data, this page and the App Store privacy details are updated before that version is released.

14. Contact

Questions about privacy: bayrambas255@icloud.com.
