Melt the treasure. Feed the dragon.

Dragon Melt is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. Your dragon breathes a pool of molten gold into a cave full of treasure. Tap a treasure that touches the pool and every connected piece of the same kind melts into it. Reach every crown before your breaths run out.

How it plays

Every breath is a choice: which treasure makes the pool grow furthest? Melt eight or more pieces at once and the dragon overheats, so the breath comes back.

Rocks, ice, powder kegs, geysers and embers change the plan from level to level. Six powers help when a level gets tight.

Between levels you open chests, build the dragon's lair and watch your hatchling grow, stage by stage.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. The game has no account and plays offline. A connection is needed only when you buy something.

How do lives work?

You have up to 5 lives. Losing a level costs one life, and a life comes back every 25 minutes. Winning never costs a life.

What are gems for?

Gems buy extra breaths, powers, life refills and faster chests. You earn them by playing (dragon growth, daily rewards, quests, chests) and you can also buy them in the Shop.

I bought something and did not receive it.

Purchases are handled by Apple. Keep the game open for a moment while you are online: an unfinished purchase is delivered the next time the game starts. If it still does not arrive, write to us with the date of the purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are decided by Apple. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I move my progress to a new device?

Progress is stored on your device and is part of your device backup (iCloud or computer). Restoring that backup on a new device brings it back. Deleting the game deletes its progress and any unused items.

How do I turn reminders off?

Open the Settings app, then Notifications, then Dragon Melt. The game always asks before it sends the first reminder.
