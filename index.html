Melt the treasure. Feed the dragon.

Dragon Melt is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. Your dragon breathes a pool of molten gold into a cave full of treasure. Tap a treasure that touches the pool and every connected piece of the same kind melts into it. Reach every crown before your breaths run out.

Frequently asked questions

Playing

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. The game has no account and plays offline. A connection is needed only when you buy something.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. The game is in English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, Traditional Chinese, Turkish, French and Arabic. This site is in English.

How do lives work?

You start with 5 lives. Losing a level costs one life, and a life comes back every 25 minutes. Winning never costs a life. Upgrading the Nest in the dragon's lair adds lives and shortens the wait.

What are gems for?

Gems buy extra breaths after a failed level, powers, life refills, and faster chests and lair upgrades. You earn them by playing (dragon growth, daily rewards, quests, chests) and you can also buy them in the Shop.

How do chests work?

You win chests by playing; they are never sold. A chest takes some time to open, and the chest window shows what it can hold before it opens. Gems can shorten the wait but do not change what is inside.

How do I turn sound or reminders off?

Sound, music and haptics are in Settings inside the game (the gear on the home screen). Reminders are in the Settings app of your device, under Notifications, then Dragon Melt. The game always asks before it sends the first reminder.

Purchases

Is the game free?

Yes. Dragon Melt is free to download and play, and it has no ads. Purchases are optional: gem packs, and bundles of gems, gold, powers and a few hours of unlimited lives.

I bought something and did not receive it.

Purchases are handled by Apple. Keep the game open for a moment while you are online: an unfinished purchase is delivered the next time the game starts. If the game says “Waiting for approval”, the purchase is waiting for a parent (Ask to Buy) or for a payment check by Apple, and it arrives as soon as that is done. If it still does not arrive, write to us with the date of the purchase.

Is there a Restore Purchases button?

No, because there is nothing to restore: everything sold in the game is used up inside the game. What you bought is part of your progress on the device, and it moves to a new device together with that progress.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are decided by Apple. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I stop purchases on my child's device?

In the Settings app of the device, Screen Time can turn in-app purchases off. With Family Sharing, Ask to Buy sends every purchase to a parent for approval.

Your progress and your data

Can I move my progress to a new device?

Progress is stored on your device and is part of your device backup (iCloud or computer). Restoring that backup on a new device, or transferring directly from the old device during setup, brings it back. Deleting the game deletes its progress and any unused items.

Does the game collect my data?

No. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the game: all of its data is on your device and goes with it. The steps are on the Data deletion page.

How it plays

Every breath is a choice: which treasure makes the pool grow furthest? Melt a big enough group in one breath and the dragon overheats, so the breath comes back. The heat meter shows how many pieces it takes.

Rocks, ice, powder kegs, geysers, embers, prisms, locks, tunnels and pedestals change the plan from level to level. Six powers help when a level gets tight.

Between levels you open chests, build the dragon's lair and watch your hatchling grow, stage by stage.
